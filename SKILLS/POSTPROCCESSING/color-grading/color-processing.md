# Color Processing

## Purpose

Color processing establishes the technical color foundation of a cinematic image through tonal balancing, color management, and dynamic range optimization.

Unlike image finishing, which enhances texture and atmosphere, or optical effects, which simulate physical lens behavior, color processing ensures that the image is visually coherent, physically believable, and emotionally appropriate before any creative stylization is applied.

Use color processing to improve color accuracy, tonal consistency, and overall image quality while supporting the intended cinematic mood.

---

# Color Correction

## Sample Prompt

Apply professional color correction to establish accurate and consistent color reproduction throughout the image. Balance white levels, remove unwanted color casts, preserve realistic skin tones, and maintain consistent exposure before introducing any creative grading.

---

## Essence

Accurate and consistent color reproduction.

---

## Characteristics

- balanced white levels
- neutral color reproduction
- natural skin tones
- consistent exposure
- corrected color casts
- smooth tonal transitions
- professional image consistency

---

## Best For

- cinematic workflows
- realistic photography
- documentaries
- narrative filmmaking
- commercial production
- multi-shot sequences

---

## Avoid When

- intentionally stylized color distortion
- surreal artistic grading
- experimental imagery

---

# White Balance

## Sample Prompt

Adjust white balance to establish accurate color temperature and neutral whites under the scene's lighting conditions. Remove unwanted warm or cool color casts while preserving the intended emotional atmosphere through subtle temperature control.

---

## Essence

Natural color temperature and believable lighting.

---

## Characteristics

- accurate color temperature
- neutral white reproduction
- warm or cool tonal adjustment
- realistic lighting balance
- natural skin tones
- controlled color neutrality

---

## Best For

- mixed lighting environments
- indoor photography
- outdoor cinematography
- realistic filmmaking
- portrait photography
- emotionally driven color temperature shifts

---

## Avoid When

- intentionally unrealistic color palettes
- abstract artistic imagery
- extreme fantasy color grading

---

# HDR

## Sample Prompt

Apply HDR processing to expand the image's dynamic range while preserving realistic highlight roll-off and shadow detail. Enhance depth, clarity, and tonal richness without introducing exaggerated contrast or unnatural processing artifacts.

---

## Essence

Expanded tonal range with preserved detail.

---

## Characteristics

- expanded dynamic range
- preserved highlights
- visible shadow detail
- balanced contrast
- enhanced tonal depth
- improved local clarity
- realistic brightness distribution

---

## Best For

- landscapes
- architecture
- dramatic environments
- sunsets
- cityscapes
- high-contrast lighting conditions

---

## Avoid When

- vintage aesthetics
- soft nostalgic imagery
- intentionally low-contrast cinematography
- dreamlike visual styles

---

# Color Processing Guidelines

- Establish technical color accuracy before applying creative stylization.
- Preserve realistic skin tones whenever appropriate.
- Maintain consistent color temperature throughout a sequence unless motivated by the narrative.
- Protect highlight and shadow detail before increasing contrast.
- Avoid excessive saturation or unnatural tonal shifts.
- Allow cinematography and lighting to define the emotional character of the image.
- Use HDR to recover detail rather than create exaggerated visual effects.

---

# Final Principle

Color processing establishes the visual foundation of the cinematic image.

Its purpose is to create accurate, balanced, and emotionally appropriate color that supports the story without drawing attention to the processing itself.

The strongest color processing is often invisible—it quietly improves realism, continuity, and cinematic quality while allowing every other visual element to work together naturally.
