# Spaghetti Western

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of Spaghetti Western as a complete cinematic movement for Seedance.

Spaghetti Western is not simply a Western setting, cowboy imagery, deserts, horses, or gunfights. It is a filmmaking philosophy built around mythic storytelling, moral ambiguity, extreme visual composition, silence, and ritualized tension.

When activated, this skill should transform the scene into a world where characters feel like legends trapped by fate, landscapes feel monumental, and every confrontation carries emotional and symbolic weight.

The final result should feel like a cinematic myth rather than a realistic historical event.

# Cinematic Law

Spaghetti Western transforms human conflict into myth.

Ordinary people become symbolic figures.
Small gestures become dramatic events.
Silence becomes dialogue.
Landscape becomes psychology.

Every creative decision should increase:

- anticipation
- isolation
- inevitability
- moral uncertainty
- mythic scale

The audience should feel that every character is approaching a destiny they cannot escape.

# Application Philosophy

Treat Spaghetti Western as a complete cinematic language.

Its influence should shape:

- pacing
- camera behavior
- composition
- landscape design
- character presentation
- lighting
- editing rhythm
- atmosphere

Prioritize tension over action.

A moment before violence should feel more important than the violence itself.

Allow:

- silence
- distance
- waiting
- staring
- environmental scale

to create dramatic intensity.

---

# Interpretation Priority

When translating Spaghetti Western into a prompt, prioritize artistic decisions in this order:

1. Mythic atmosphere
2. Visual composition
3. Character symbolism
4. Tension and pacing
5. Landscape and environment
6. Camera behavior
7. Lighting
8. Editing

If a choice makes the scene more spectacular but less mythic, choose restraint.

# Camera

Camera language should create tension through observation and scale.

Favor:

- extreme close-ups of eyes and faces
- slow push-ins
- long static compositions
- wide landscape shots
- low-angle hero framing
- deliberate tracking shots
- patient camera movement
- dramatic reveals
- visual contrast between human figures and vast environments

The camera should make small actions feel monumental.

Avoid:

- fast handheld action coverage
- constant movement
- modern action cinematography
- flashy camera choreography

---

# Composition

Composition should create mythic isolation.

Favor:

- vast empty landscapes
- tiny figures against huge environments
- strong horizons
- symmetrical confrontations
- visual distance between characters
- dramatic silhouettes
- architectural framing
- empty spaces before conflict

Characters should often appear insignificant compared with their surroundings.

---

# Character Presentation

Characters should feel like archetypes rather than ordinary individuals.

Common figures include:

- the lone stranger
- the revenge seeker
- the bounty hunter
- the outlaw
- the corrupt authority figure
- the morally conflicted survivor

Favor:

- restrained expressions
- unreadable faces
- controlled body language
- minimal dialogue
- internal conflict

Avoid:

- cheerful adventure heroes
- exaggerated comedy
- emotionally transparent characters

---

# Lighting

Lighting should feel natural yet dramatic.

Favor:

- harsh sunlight
- golden-hour landscapes
- deep shadows
- strong silhouettes
- dusty atmosphere
- backlighting
- dramatic contrast
- warm earth tones
- natural environmental light

Avoid:

- glossy Hollywood lighting
- artificial studio illumination
- overly colorful fantasy lighting

---

# Editing

Editing should build anticipation.

Favor:

- slow pacing
- long pauses
- gradual escalation
- deliberate rhythm
- extended moments before action
- visual suspense

Avoid:

- rapid action editing
- constant cutting
- modern blockbuster pacing

The waiting should be as dramatic as the confrontation.

---

# Atmosphere

Spaghetti Western creates a world of loneliness and inevitability.

Common elements include:

- desert winds
- dust
- abandoned towns
- empty streets
- distant horizons
- silence
- tension
- isolation
- fate
- revenge
- moral ambiguity
- forgotten landscapes

The environment should feel ancient, harsh, and emotionally symbolic.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid reducing Spaghetti Western to cowboy aesthetics.

It is not simply:

- horses
- guns
- deserts
- cowboy hats
- saloons

Avoid:

- generic action Westerns
- heroic adventure tone
- fast-paced gunfights
- comedic cowboy imagery
- clean fantasy landscapes
- modern action choreography
- excessive dialogue

If the scene feels like an adventure movie rather than a mythic confrontation, it has moved away from Spaghetti Western.
