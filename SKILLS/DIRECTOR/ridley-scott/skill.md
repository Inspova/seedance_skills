# Ridley Scott

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of Ridley Scott's filmmaking style for Seedance.

Ridley Scott's style should never be treated as simply atmospheric haze, epic landscapes, futuristic design, or large-scale production value. Instead, it represents a complete filmmaking philosophy built around immersive world-building, environmental realism, tactile production design, cinematic atmosphere, and visually rich storytelling.

When activated, this skill should transform the visual language into a world that feels physically real, historically or culturally authentic, and fully inhabited.

The final image should feel as though the audience has entered an existing world rather than a constructed movie set.

# When to Use

Apply this skill when the user explicitly requests Ridley Scott or when the creative objective naturally aligns with his cinematic philosophy.

Typical indicators include:

- science fiction
- historical epics
- military drama
- immersive fantasy worlds
- dystopian environments
- industrial settings
- ancient civilizations
- environmental storytelling
- atmospheric realism
- large-scale human conflict

Do not activate this skill simply because the user requests:

- fog
- dramatic lighting
- large landscapes
- futuristic architecture
- epic imagery

Atmosphere alone does not constitute Ridley Scott's style.

---

# Application Philosophy

Treat Ridley Scott's style as a complete visual system rather than a collection of aesthetic effects.

Its influence should consistently shape:

- world-building
- production design
- environment
- lighting
- composition
- camera behavior
- atmosphere
- visual storytelling

Every artistic decision should reinforce:

- physical realism
- environmental depth
- lived-in authenticity
- cultural richness
- cinematic immersion

The world itself should communicate history, technology, social structure, and human presence.

Avoid creating beautiful environments that feel empty or artificial.

---

# Interpretation Priority

When translating Ridley Scott's style into a prompt, prioritize artistic decisions in the following order:

1. World-building authenticity
2. Production design
3. Environmental storytelling
4. Lighting atmosphere
5. Composition
6. Camera behavior
7. Color treatment
8. Visual effects

If a choice makes the world more spectacular but less believable, prioritize authenticity.

- immersive environments

- lived-in production design
- layered compositions
- atmospheric depth
- practical lighting
- realistic textures
- architectural detail
- environmental storytelling
- cinematic scale

The resulting prompt should still feel Ridley Scott-inspired even if the director's name is removed.

---

# Core Visual Language

Ridley Scott's imagery combines realism with visual richness.

Common characteristics include:

- detailed environments
- monumental architecture
- industrial complexity
- ancient civilizations
- futuristic cities
- functional machinery
- weathered surfaces
- layered backgrounds
- atmospheric depth
- human-scale details within massive worlds
- strong visual contrast

Every environment should feel like it has a history.

---

# Camera

Camera movement should be deliberate and immersive.

Favor:

- slow controlled movement
- sweeping environmental shots
- deliberate tracking shots
- observational camera placement
- wide compositions
- layered foreground and background
- movement through physical spaces
- carefully composed imagery

Avoid:

- excessive camera movement
- artificial spectacle shots
- weightless digital movement

The camera should explore the world rather than simply display it.

---

# Lighting

Lighting is one of Ridley Scott's strongest visual signatures.

Favor:

- practical light sources
- atmospheric haze
- motivated illumination
- strong directional light
- deep shadows
- shafts of light
- firelight
- industrial lighting
- natural environmental lighting
- subtle contrast

Light should reveal the physical properties of the world.

Avoid:

- artificial beauty lighting
- clean studio illumination
- unrealistic fantasy lighting

---

# Composition

Composition should emphasize depth, scale, and environmental storytelling.

Favor:

- layered environments
- foreground objects
- architectural framing
- strong silhouettes
- characters integrated into surroundings
- large spaces with human presence
- visual density
- balanced complexity

The environment should be as important as the characters.

---

# Production Design

Production design is central to Ridley Scott's filmmaking language.

Favor:

- worn materials
- functional objects
- realistic architecture
- cultural details
- industrial textures
- mechanical complexity
- historical authenticity
- environmental imperfections
- signs of human occupation

Avoid:

- empty futuristic spaces
- generic fantasy environments
- decorative design without purpose

Every object should suggest a larger world.

---

# Atmosphere

Ridley Scott's atmosphere combines beauty with uncertainty.

Common elements include:

- mystery
- isolation
- grandeur
- danger
- melancholy
- discovery
- historical weight
- environmental tension
- human vulnerability

The audience should feel immersed inside the world.

---

# Style Boundaries

Avoid reducing Ridley Scott to:

- fog
- epic landscapes
- futuristic cities
- medieval battles
- dramatic lighting

Avoid:

- empty spectacle
- artificial CGI worlds
- sterile environments
- generic fantasy aesthetics
- unrealistic production design
- excessive visual effects

The defining quality is not scale.

It is believable worlds.

---

# Default Behavior

If Ridley Scott's style is not requested and is not clearly appropriate for the narrative, do not apply this skill.

Maintain visual neutrality and allow other director styles or cinematic movements to guide the scene.

---

# Final Principle

Ridley Scott's filmmaking style is a philosophy of immersive world creation.

Its success is measured not by visual grandeur alone, but by whether every cinematic decision—especially environment, production design, lighting, and atmosphere—creates the feeling of a complete world with its own history, culture, and physical reality.
