# Psychology

## Purpose

This module governs the internal psychological state of characters.

Its goal is to create believable, emotionally coherent characters whose thoughts, feelings, and personalities influence their actions naturally.

Psychology should drive behavior rather than merely describing it. Characters should react according to who they are and what they are experiencing.

Consult the Psychology library before inventing psychological descriptions.

- `emotions.md`
- `personality.md`

---

# Intent

Treat every character as a psychologically believable individual.

Emotions should arise naturally from context.

Personality should remain consistent throughout the story unless intentional character development occurs.

---

# Psychology Contract

Psychological behavior should obey the following principles.

- emotions should fit the situation
- personality remains relatively stable
- emotional reactions should vary in intensity
- internal state influences external behavior
- contradictions should be intentional rather than random

Avoid emotional responses that feel exaggerated without narrative justification.

---

# Decision Process

Before describing a character's psychology, determine:

### 1. Who is this character?

Consult:

- `personality.md`

Examples:

- optimistic
- analytical
- impulsive
- compassionate
- arrogant

---

### 2. What is the character feeling?

Consult:

- `emotions.md`

Examples:

- hopeful
- anxious
- relieved
- grieving
- determined

---

### 3. How should those emotions influence behavior?

Emotions should affect:

- decisions
- dialogue
- pacing
- body language
- interactions
- confidence
- risk-taking

---

# Psychology Hierarchy

Prioritize:

1. Narrative consistency
2. Personality consistency
3. Emotional realism
4. Character development
5. Dramatic impact

---

# Continuity

Maintain consistency for:

- personality
- emotional progression
- reactions
- beliefs
- decision-making

Avoid sudden unexplained emotional shifts.

---

# Conflict Resolution

If multiple psychological instructions conflict, prioritize:

1. story context
2. established personality
3. current emotional state
4. dramatic effect

---

# Default Behaviour

If psychology is unspecified:

- assume emotionally balanced behavior
- avoid emotional extremes
- use realistic reactions
- maintain personality consistency

---

# Output

Psychological descriptions should specify, where appropriate:

- personality traits
- current emotional state
- emotional intensity
- behavioral tendencies
- internal conflict
- emotional progression

Psychology should explain why a character behaves as they do, not merely label their emotions.
