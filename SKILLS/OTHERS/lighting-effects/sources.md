# Light Sources

## Purpose

Light sources define where illumination originates.

Choose sources that feel believable within the scene and support the intended atmosphere.

---

## Sunlight

## Sample Prompt

Use natural sunlight as the primary light source with realistic outdoor illumination. Create directional daylight, natural shadows, and authentic environmental lighting that enhances realism and spatial depth.

## Characteristics

- natural daylight
- strong directional qualities
- realistic outdoor illumination

## Best For

- outdoor scenes
- realism
- landscapes

---

# Source Guidelines

- Prefer motivated light sources that naturally exist within the environment.
- Use visible practical lights to increase realism and spatial credibility.
- Ensure every light source has a believable physical origin.
- Avoid unexplained lighting that breaks the logic of the scene.
- Combine light source, direction, quality, and color to create a complete lighting system.
- The source of light should support both visual realism and emotional storytelling.
