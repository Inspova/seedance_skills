# Hayao Miyazaki

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of Hayao Miyazaki's cinematic style for Seedance.

Hayao Miyazaki's style is not simply anime, hand-drawn animation, or whimsical fantasy. It is a complete artistic language built around humanistic storytelling, living natural worlds, quiet emotional moments, childlike wonder, environmental harmony, and beautifully handcrafted animation.

When activated, this skill should transform the visual presentation into a hand-painted animated world inspired by Hayao Miyazaki while preserving the original narrative, characters, actions, and emotional intent.

The world itself should feel alive. Nature, weather, architecture, light, and movement should become active participants in the storytelling rather than passive backgrounds.

The goal is not simply to create beautiful animation.

The goal is to create a world where wonder exists within everyday life, and where emotional truth emerges through quiet observation, nature, and human connection.

# When to Use

Activate this skill whenever the user explicitly requests Hayao Miyazaki or when the creative objective naturally aligns with heartfelt animated fantasy and nature-centered storytelling.

Typical indicators include:

- hand-drawn animation
- whimsical fantasy
- magical realism
- coming-of-age stories
- peaceful adventures
- nature
- environmental storytelling
- childhood wonder
- emotional fantasy
- cozy worlds
- flying adventures
- gentle magic

Do not activate this skill solely because the user requests:

- anime
- Japanese animation
- fantasy
- watercolor backgrounds

These elements alone do not represent Hayao Miyazaki's artistic philosophy.

# Visual Transformation

Transform the original scene into a hand-painted animated film inspired by Hayao Miyazaki.

Characters, environments, props, weather, and natural elements should all adopt the handcrafted visual language of traditional feature animation.

Preserve:

- characters
- actions
- environments
- story
- emotional intent
- camera intent

Translate them into a handcrafted animated world rather than replacing them with imagery from existing Miyazaki films.

The transformation should affect the artistic medium while preserving the narrative.

---

# Style Boundaries

Avoid reducing Hayao Miyazaki's style to:

- anime aesthetics
- cute creatures
- fantasy settings
- watercolor backgrounds
- Studio Ghibli references alone

These are only surface characteristics.

The defining qualities are:

- humanistic storytelling
- living environments
- emotional sincerity
- harmony between people and nature
- handcrafted animation
- quiet wonder

---

# Integration

When multiple skills are active, Hayao Miyazaki should primarily influence:

- artistic medium
- environment design
- natural world
- atmosphere
- animation style
- emotional tone

Other compatible skills may continue to influence genre, cinematography, lighting, or narrative, provided they remain compatible with Miyazaki's gentle humanistic philosophy.

---

# Narrative Preservation

Preserve the original:

- characters
- actions
- story
- setting
- camera intent
- emotional relationships

Only transform the artistic presentation.

Do not replace the narrative with scenes, creatures, or locations from Hayao Miyazaki's films.

Instead, reinterpret the existing scene as though it were produced as an original Hayao Miyazaki animated feature.

---

# Default Behavior

If Hayao Miyazaki's style is neither explicitly requested nor naturally appropriate for the artistic goal, do not activate this skill.

Maintain visual neutrality and allow other artistic styles to determine the final appearance.

---

# Final Principle

Hayao Miyazaki's cinema is a complete artistic philosophy rather than an anime aesthetic.

Every creative decision should transform the world into a handcrafted animated landscape filled with living nature, quiet emotional storytelling, gentle wonder, and human warmth while preserving the original narrative, characters, and emotional truth.
