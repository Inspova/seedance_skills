# Terrence Malick

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of Terrence Malick's filmmaking style for Seedance.

Terrence Malick's style should never be treated as simply golden-hour cinematography, nature photography, or slow poetic imagery. Instead, it serves as a unified filmmaking philosophy built upon poetic realism, spiritual contemplation, natural beauty, and emotionally immersive visual storytelling.

Its purpose is to translate Terrence Malick's visual philosophy into coherent filmmaking decisions that reinforce wonder, human vulnerability, memory, and the relationship between people and the natural world.

When activated, this skill should transform the visual language into an intimate, contemplative cinematic experience where camera movement, lighting, composition, and atmosphere express emotional and philosophical reflection.

The result should feel like a moment of human existence being observed with curiosity, reverence, and emotional openness.

# When to Use

Activate this skill whenever the user explicitly requests Terrence Malick or when the creative objective naturally aligns with poetic, contemplative, and spiritually reflective filmmaking.

Typical indicators include:

- nature
- landscapes
- childhood memories
- family relationships
- spiritual reflection
- personal journeys
- emotional introspection
- human connection
- existential themes
- pastoral environments
- poetic storytelling

Do not activate this skill solely because the user requests:

- golden-hour lighting
- lens flare
- outdoor scenery
- slow motion
- beautiful landscapes

These elements alone do not constitute Terrence Malick's style.

# Application Philosophy

Treat Terrence Malick's style as a complete cinematic language rather than a collection of visual techniques.

Its influence should extend across:

- camera behavior
- composition
- lighting
- color
- atmosphere
- environmental relationships
- pacing
- emotional storytelling

Every artistic decision should reinforce:

- wonder
- intimacy
- natural beauty
- human vulnerability
- philosophical reflection

Favor organic movement, natural environments, and emotional observation over controlled staging or dramatic spectacle.

Avoid reducing the style to warm colors, nature imagery, or visual beauty alone.

# Visual Translation

Never rely solely on the phrase:

Instead, translate the filmmaker's philosophy into explicit cinematic decisions.

Whenever appropriate, describe:

- floating observational camera movement
- natural light and backlighting
- poetic environmental compositions
- organic handheld movement
- intimate human moments
- expansive landscapes
- contemplative pacing
- emotional realism
- spiritual atmosphere
- connection between characters and nature

The resulting prompt should still feel like Terrence Malick even if the director's name is removed.

---

# Style Boundaries

Avoid superficial imitation.

Terrence Malick's style is defined by poetic realism, spiritual contemplation, and emotional intimacy—not simply:

- golden-hour photography
- lens flare
- slow camera movement
- beautiful landscapes
- nature imagery

These elements are meaningful only when they support deeper emotional or philosophical reflection.

Avoid:

- empty visual beauty
- artificial sentimentality
- excessive slow motion
- decorative landscapes
- dramatic emotional exaggeration
- overly polished commercial imagery

The goal is not to make reality look beautiful.

The goal is to reveal beauty already existing within reality.

---

# Default Behavior

If Terrence Malick's style is neither explicitly requested nor naturally appropriate for the narrative, do not activate this skill.

Maintain visual neutrality and allow other director styles or cinematic movements to determine the overall visual language.

---

# Final Principle

Terrence Malick's style is a complete cinematic philosophy rather than a collection of visual effects.

Its success is measured not by golden light or natural landscapes, but by whether every creative decision—especially camera movement, composition, lighting, and atmosphere—creates a feeling of wonder, contemplation, emotional intimacy, and connection between humanity and the natural world.
