# Light Quality

## Purpose

Light quality defines the visual character of illumination.

It influences texture, contrast, mood, and how subjects are perceived. Choose light quality based on the emotional intent of the scene rather than technical preference.

---

## Soft Light

## Sample Prompt

Use soft lighting with smooth transitions between highlights and shadows. Maintain gentle contrast, flattering skin tones, and a natural appearance to create an intimate, peaceful, and emotionally warm atmosphere.

## Characteristics

- smooth transitions between light and shadow
- flattering skin tones
- gentle contrast
- natural appearance

## Best For

- portraits
- romance
- beauty
- dialogue
- peaceful scenes

---

## Hard Light

## Sample Prompt

Use hard lighting with strong directional illumination and clearly defined shadows. Emphasize texture, contrast, and dramatic shapes to create a powerful and intense visual atmosphere.

## Characteristics

- sharp shadows
- strong contrast
- emphasizes texture
- dramatic appearance

## Best For

- suspense
- action
- noir
- intense drama

---

## Diffused Light

## Sample Prompt

Use diffused lighting with evenly scattered illumination and reduced shadow intensity. Create balanced exposure, soft transitions, and a realistic natural appearance similar to overcast daylight or large soft light sources.

## Characteristics

- evenly scattered illumination
- minimal harsh shadows
- balanced exposure

## Best For

- overcast scenes
- interiors
- product shots
- natural realism

---

## Directional Light

## Sample Prompt

Use directional lighting with a clearly defined light source creating controlled highlights and shadows. Sculpt the subject through intentional contrast and emphasize form, depth, and cinematic structure.

## Characteristics

- clearly defined light direction
- pronounced highlights and shadows
- sculpted appearance

## Best For

- dramatic portraits
- cinematic storytelling
- emphasizing form

---

## High-Key Lighting

## Sample Prompt

Use high-key lighting with bright overall exposure, soft contrast, and minimal shadows. Create a clean, optimistic, and polished visual style with a sense of openness and clarity.

## Characteristics

- bright overall exposure
- low contrast
- few shadows

## Best For

- comedy
- lifestyle
- fashion
- commercial work

---

## Low-Key Lighting

## Sample Prompt

Use low-key lighting with dark exposure, deep shadows, and strong contrast. Emphasize selective illumination, mystery, and dramatic atmosphere while preserving important subject details.

## Characteristics

- dark overall exposure
- deep shadows
- high contrast

## Best For

- horror
- thriller
- mystery
- film noir

## Rim Light

## Sample Prompt

Use rim lighting with a bright light source positioned behind or around the edges of the subject to create a glowing outline. Enhance separation from the background and create a dramatic, cinematic emphasis on the subject's shape.

## Characteristics

- bright outline around the subject
- strong separation from background
- edge highlights

## Best For

- hero shots
- portraits
- dramatic reveals

---

## Silhouette

## Sample Prompt

Use silhouette lighting by exposing the background brighter than the subject, reducing the subject into a dark shape with minimal visible detail. Create strong visual symbolism, mystery, and emotional impact through simplified forms.

## Characteristics

- subject appears dark
- background dominates exposure
- simplified visual shape

## Best For

- mystery
- symbolism
- sunsets
- emotional endings

---

## Volumetric Light

## Sample Prompt

Use volumetric lighting with visible beams of light passing through atmospheric elements such as fog, dust, smoke, or mist. Create depth, cinematic atmosphere, and a sense of environmental scale.

## Characteristics

- visible light rays
- atmospheric depth
- cinematic ambience

## Best For

- forests
- churches
- fog
- fantasy

---

## Dappled Light

## Sample Prompt

Use dappled lighting with irregular patterns of sunlight and shadow created by natural obstacles such as leaves, trees, or architectural structures. Create an organic, textured lighting pattern with a natural outdoor feeling.

## Characteristics

- irregular patches of sunlight and shadow
- organic patterns
- natural contrast

## Best For

- forests
- gardens
- outdoor scenes

---

# Lighting Guidelines

- Match light quality to the emotional tone of the scene.
- Prefer soft light for intimacy, beauty, and emotional warmth.
- Prefer hard light for tension, conflict, and dramatic contrast.
- Use low-key lighting to create mystery, depth, and psychological atmosphere.
- Use high-key lighting for optimism, clarity, and openness.
- Combine light quality with direction and color to create a complete lighting system.
- Avoid choosing lighting styles only for visual appearance; every lighting decision should support the story and emotional intent.
