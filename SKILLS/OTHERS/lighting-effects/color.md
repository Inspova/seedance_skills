# Light Color

## Purpose

Light color influences mood, atmosphere, and emotional perception.

Choose color temperature and hue to support the narrative rather than visual style alone.

---

## Warm Light

## Sample Prompt

Use warm lighting with orange, amber, and golden tones to create an emotionally inviting atmosphere. Soft warm illumination should enhance skin tones, create intimacy, and evoke feelings of comfort, nostalgia, and human connection.

## Characteristics

- orange
- amber
- golden

## Emotion

- comfort
- intimacy
- nostalgia
- hope

---

## Neutral Light

## Sample Prompt

Use balanced neutral white lighting that preserves natural colors and realistic skin tones. Maintain accurate color reproduction and a truthful visual appearance, creating a sense of realism, clarity, and authenticity.

## Characteristics

- balanced white light
- natural appearance

## Emotion

- realism
- clarity
- honesty

---

## Cool Light

## Sample Prompt

Use cool blue and cyan lighting to create emotional distance and atmospheric depth. Cool illumination should emphasize quietness, isolation, mystery, and melancholy while maintaining a controlled cinematic mood.

## Characteristics

- blue
- cyan

## Emotion

- isolation
- calm
- melancholy
- mystery

---

## Mixed Lighting

## Sample Prompt

Combine multiple color temperatures within the same environment, allowing warm and cool light sources to interact naturally. Use practical sources such as street lights, windows, lamps, and screens to create realistic cinematic contrast and layered atmosphere.

## Best For

- city nights
- interiors
- realistic environments

# Color Guidelines

- Warm light generally creates emotional intimacy and human connection.
- Cool light generally creates emotional distance, calmness, or mystery.
- Neutral light supports realism and visual honesty.
- Colored lighting should have a clear narrative or environmental motivation.
- Avoid excessive color mixing unless intentionally creating a stylized atmosphere.
- Consider the relationship between light color and surrounding materials, skin tones, and environment.
- Lighting color should support emotion before creating visual decoration.
