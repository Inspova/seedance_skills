# Cinema Vérité

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of Cinema Vérité as a complete cinematic movement for Seedance.

Cinema Vérité is not simply handheld camerawork, documentary aesthetics, or imperfect image quality. It is a complete cinematic language built around observation, authenticity, spontaneity, and emotional truth.

The camera does not create reality. It discovers reality.

Camera behavior, composition, lighting, performance, production design, sound, and editing should work together to create the feeling that the audience is witnessing genuine events unfolding without artificial control.

When activated, this skill should transform the entire visual language into observational filmmaking where authenticity takes priority over cinematic perfection.

# Cinematic Law

Cinema Vérité transforms filmmaking from observation into experience.

The camera should behave like an invisible witness entering an already existing reality.

It should not arrange moments.
It should discover them.

Imperfections, interruptions, spontaneous reactions, and ordinary details should become part of the cinematic language.

Every creative decision should preserve the illusion that life is unfolding naturally beyond the camera's control.

---

# When to Use

Activate this skill whenever the user explicitly requests Cinema Vérité or when the creative objective naturally aligns with observational realism and documentary-inspired storytelling.

Typical indicators include:

- documentary realism
- observational filmmaking
- slice of life
- social realism
- intimate human stories
- coming-of-age drama
- family relationships
- everyday environments
- authentic performances
- immersive realism
- behind-the-scenes perspectives
- journalistic storytelling
- human-interest stories

Do not activate this skill solely because the user requests:

- handheld camera movement
- natural lighting
- realistic visuals

Handheld photography alone does not constitute Cinema Vérité.

---

# Application Philosophy

Treat Cinema Vérité as a complete cinematic philosophy rather than a collection of documentary techniques.

Its influence should consistently shape:

- camera behavior
- performance authenticity
- composition
- lighting
- production design
- atmosphere
- editing rhythm
- environmental sound

Every artistic decision should prioritize:

- authenticity over beauty
- observation over control
- spontaneity over choreography
- human behavior over visual perfection

The audience should feel like an invisible observer rather than someone watching a constructed performance.

---

# Interpretation Priority

When translating Cinema Vérité into a prompt, prioritize artistic decisions in this order:

1. Performance authenticity
2. Camera behavior
3. Environmental realism
4. Lighting authenticity
5. Composition
6. Production design
7. Editing rhythm

If conflicts occur with other stylistic choices, preserve the earlier priorities.

A perfectly composed image that feels staged is less authentic than an imperfect image that feels real.

# Authenticity Rules

Cinema Vérité should feel discovered rather than designed.

Favor:

- natural interruptions
- unscripted gestures
- imperfect timing
- ordinary behavior
- real-world clutter
- environmental noise
- casual movement
- natural pauses
- incomplete moments

Avoid:

- posed performances
- artificial emotional beats
- overly beautiful compositions
- perfect blocking
- commercial polish
- staged reactions

Reality should feel more important than cinematic elegance.

---

# Style Boundaries

Avoid reducing Cinema Vérité to visual imperfection alone.

Cinema Vérité is not defined solely by:

- handheld camera
- shaky footage
- natural lighting
- documentary appearance
- rough image quality

These techniques only matter when they reinforce genuine observation.

Avoid:

- "fake documentary" aesthetics
- polished advertisement-style realism
- dramatic cinematic lighting
- excessive camera shake
- artificial imperfections added for style
- overly controlled performances

The goal is not to imitate documentary appearance.

The goal is to recreate documentary truthfulness.

---

# Integration

Cinema Vérité should establish the dominant visual language whenever active.

When combined with other filmmaking skills, Cinema Vérité should control:

- camera behavior
- performance style
- realism level
- atmosphere

Other styles may influence subject matter or emotional tone but should not remove the observational nature of the movement.

---

# Default Behavior

If Cinema Vérité is neither explicitly requested nor naturally appropriate for the narrative, do not activate this skill.

Maintain visual neutrality and allow other cinematic movement skills to determine the overall style.

---

# Final Principle

Cinema Vérité is the art of making cinema feel like reality rather than making reality look like cinema.

Its success is measured not by handheld movement or documentary appearance, but by whether every cinematic decision creates the convincing illusion that the audience is witnessing authentic human moments unfolding naturally.
