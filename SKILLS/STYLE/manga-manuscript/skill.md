# Manga Manuscript

## Purpose

This skill governs the application of authentic Japanese manga manuscript aesthetics for Seedance.

Manga manuscripts are not simply black-and-white illustrations, anime drawings, or pencil sketches. They are a complete artistic language built around expressive ink linework, monochrome rendering, screentones, dramatic composition, and visual storytelling designed for publication.

When activated, this skill should transform every character, object, environment, and visual element into a professionally illustrated manga manuscript while preserving the original narrative, characters, actions, and emotional intent.

The result should resemble finished pages from a professionally published Japanese manga rather than concept art or rough sketches.

The goal is not merely to remove color.

The goal is to recreate the visual language of authentic manga artwork.

This is the defining feature of the style.

Transform every visual element into authentic manga artwork.

Characters, environments, props, lighting, and effects should all be rendered using professional monochrome manga techniques.

Preserve:

- characters
- actions
- environments
- story
- emotional intent
- camera intent

Translate them into authentic manga illustration rather than replacing them with imagery from an existing series.

The transformation should affect the artistic medium, not the narrative.

# Style Boundaries

Avoid reducing manga manuscripts to:

- black-and-white filters
- anime line art
- pencil sketches
- rough concept art
- Western comic books

These are only surface characteristics.

The defining qualities are:

- expressive ink rendering
- professional manga craftsmanship
- monochrome visual storytelling
- varied line weight
- screentones
- dramatic black-and-white contrast

# Narrative Preservation

Preserve the original:

- characters
- actions
- story
- setting
- camera intent
- emotional relationships

Only transform the artistic presentation.

Do not replace the narrative with scenes, characters, or designs from existing manga.

Instead, reinterpret the existing scene as though it were illustrated for publication as an original Japanese manga.
